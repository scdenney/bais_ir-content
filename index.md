    <h2 id="course-arc">The course arc</h2>
    <p>The sequence begins with the discipline's major explanatory traditions, then turns to identity and social change before closing with planetary futures. Each page gives an overview of the lecture, its core readings and any classroom inquiry. It ends with conclusions students should be able to defend.</p>
